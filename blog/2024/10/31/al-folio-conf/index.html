Elevate Your Conference Website with al-folio-conf: A Jekyll Theme 🚀

For conference organizers seeking a user-friendly website, al-folio-conf offers a possible solution. This Jekyll theme, a fork of the popular al-folio, combines simplicity, elegance, and functionality, making it perfect for showcasing your event. (Of course, I use al-folio for this website as well 😉)

Getting Started 🚀

Visit the GitHub repository for detailed instructions on setting up and customizing your site. Examples and templates are available to help you launch your conference website efficiently.

Real-World Application 🌍

As the theme of choice for SE2025, al-folio-conf has shown its potential in creating a seamless and professional conference website. Check out the SE2025 site here to see it in action!
