Dominik Fuchß

Researcher at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany.

I work on using informal artifacts — documentation, requirements, plain prose — for automatic traceability link recovery and consistency checking. Lately that means putting large language models and retrieval-augmented generation to work on traceability tasks.
